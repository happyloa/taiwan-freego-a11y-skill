PASS if every finding uses one of four base statuses, Pass, Fail, Pending or Not applicable, or their Chinese equivalents (通過, 不通過 or 不符合, 待測 or 待確認, 不適用), with evidence for each. A short qualifier in parentheses, such as 通過（原始碼檢查） or 待測（高風險）, is fine. The reply must not claim that Freego, a screen reader or a browser test was actually run. FAIL if it uses other statuses (for example "partial", "warning" or a score) or claims such a run.
